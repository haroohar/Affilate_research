Referral amounts are what Mercor shows on each listing. "Fixed" rewards are paid in full after a successful placement. "Earnings share" pays 20% of what your referral earns, capped at 4× their hourly rate on their first accepted role. Commitment comes from Mercor's hours-per-week field, or from the posting text when that field is empty. "Flexible" means no weekly hours are stated, so check the posting before promising someone a light load. Mercor pays the reward only when the referred person lives in a country it supports, and the person must sign up through your link and confirm you as their referrer.
